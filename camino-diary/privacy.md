Camino Diary is a trip planner and diary for the Camino de Santiago, made by Seanchas (TODO: legal name as registered
in the Play Console). This policy says what the app does with your information.

**In short: your plans, diary pages, photos and stamps stay on your phone. We don't collect them, and we can't see
them.** There is no account and no sign-in.

## What the app keeps, and where

Everything you put into Camino Diary is stored on your phone, inside the app's own storage:

- your trips, stages and plans;
- your diary pages;
- the photos and stamp images you add (the app keeps its own copy of each);
- your settings.

None of it is sent to us. The app has no servers of its own.

**Photos.** You choose photos through Android's photo picker, which gives the app only the photos you pick. The
picker removes the location from those photos. A photo taken with the Camera button in the app is taken by your
camera app, and may keep the location your camera app records. Camino Diary does not read photo locations, and it
does not ask for permission to use your location.

## Copies you choose to make

Your data only leaves the app when you, or Android at your request, copy it somewhere:

- **Android backup.** If backup is on for your phone, Android copies your trips, pages and settings (not your photos)
  to your Google account, and can copy everything, photos included, when you move to a new phone. Google handles this
  under your Google account and its terms; we can't see it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.
- **Automatic copy.** If you turn it on in Settings, the app saves a copy of each trip, photos included, into a folder
  you choose. If that folder is in a cloud service such as Google Drive, that service's terms apply to it.
- **Export.** When you export a trip, the app makes a file and you choose where it goes or who it's shared with.

will never see your card or payment details.

## Contact

If you email us (for example with Send feedback in Settings), we receive your email address and what you write. We
use them only to reply, and we don't add you to any list.

## What we don't do

- No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
- No selling or sharing of your information.
- No tracking across other apps or websites.

## Deleting your data

Deleting a trip in the app deletes its pages, photos and stamps. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on
your phone. Copies you made yourself (exports, the automatic copy's folder) and your Android backup are yours to
delete; your Google account settings show and delete the backup.

## Children

Camino Diary is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone, children included.

## Your rights

Because we don't hold any of your information, there is normally nothing for us to give you, correct or delete. If
you've emailed us and want that email deleted, ask and we will delete it. If you're in the EU or the UK, you also have
the right to complain to your data protection authority.
