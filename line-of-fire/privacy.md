# Line of Fire privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Line of Fire is a stickman shooter made by Seanchas, in Ireland. This policy says what the app does with your information.

**In short: your progress stays on your phone. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking across other apps.**

## What stays on your phone

Your save (stages cleared, scores, coins, unlocks, settings) is stored in the app's own storage. We never receive it.

**Android backup.** If backup is on for your phone, Android may copy your save to your Google account so it can be restored on a new phone. Google handles that under its own terms; we can't see it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

## Crash reports

<!-- WHEN Sentry ships: confirm the opt-in wording, the retention period and the event fields below match the build. -->
If you allow it, the app sends crash and error reports to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), which acts as our processor. Reports are optional and can be switched off in Settings. If you are offline, reports wait on your phone and are sent next time you are online and playing.

A report holds: the error message and stack, the app version, the stage and mode you were playing, your phone model and Android version, and a random install ID the app makes. It holds no name, email, Google account, device ID, save contents or purchase information, and we ask Sentry not to store IP addresses.

## Purchases

<!-- WHEN billing ships: this section describes the one-time "Full Game" unlock. Update if the product list changes. -->
The app may offer a one-time purchase to unlock the full game. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details; Google tells the app whether the purchase is owned.

## Permissions

The app uses the internet only for the optional crash reports above. It does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

## Children

The app is a cartoon-violence action game and is not aimed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Your choices and contact

You can switch off crash reports in Settings, turn off Android backup, or uninstall the app, which removes the save from your phone. To ask about data, contact: seanchas.apps@gmail.com

## Changes

When the app starts sending data somewhere new, this policy changes in the same release, with a new date above. The history is in this site's git repository.
