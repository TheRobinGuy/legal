# Delete your Wild Swim Log data

This page is for Wild Swim Log users who want their Cloud sync data deleted. The data we can hold is the copy of your
swim logs that Cloud sync makes, a random sync identity, and, if you linked one for account recovery, your email
address. Photos and voice notes are never uploaded.

## The quick way: in the app

Open Wild Swim Log → Settings → Cloud sync → **Delete my cloud data**. This deletes your swims in the cloud, your
sync identity and any linked email. Swims stay on your phone unless you delete them too.

## If you can't open the app

If you lost your phone, or uninstalled the app, email **seanchas.apps@gmail.com** from the address you linked in Settings →
Cloud sync → Account recovery, with the subject "Delete my Wild Swim Log data". We will:

1. reply to confirm it is you (we may ask you to sign in with the one-time code the app sends to that address);
2. delete your cloud swims, your sync identity and your email; and
3. confirm by email within 30 days.

## If you never linked an email

Without a linked email there is nothing that connects a backup to you, so we cannot find it to delete it. It holds
only swim logs under a random identity with no name. If you reinstall the app and want it gone, the in-app button
above is the only way to reach it, and it works only for the identity on that phone.

## Other data

- **Purchases:** Google Play and RevenueCat keep purchase records as the law requires. Cancelling a subscription is
  done in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
- **Crash reports and usage signals** carry a random identifier and no swim text. Ask us to delete those too and we
  will remove what we can match to your app user ID.
