## Safety: the app gives information, not advice

**Swimming outdoors can be dangerous, especially in cold water, at night, alone, or after heavy rain.** Wild Swim Log
shows information from other sources. It is not safety advice, and it can be wrong, late or missing.

- **Bathing-water ratings** are a yearly classification of a designated beach or lake, worked out from past samples.
  A good rating does not mean the water is clean or safe today.
- **Live checks** (storm overflows, pollution-risk forecasts, restriction notices) come from public feeds run by
  others. They cover only some places, they can be delayed or down, and many discharges are never reported. A place
  with no warning is not a place known to be safe, and "no live checks here" means exactly that.
- **Weather, tide and water temperature** are forecasts, predictions or your own readings. Tide times are
  predictions from models and can be out by minutes or more; they are not for navigation.
- **Cold-water, wetsuit and similar notes** are general information, not medical advice.

You decide whether to swim. Check conditions on site, follow local signs and lifeguards, swim within your ability, and
don't rely on the app alone. In an emergency call your local emergency number.
